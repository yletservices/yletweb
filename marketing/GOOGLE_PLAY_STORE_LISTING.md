## 6. Graphic Asset Requirements Checklist

When uploading visual assets in Google Play Console:

- [ ] **App Icon:** `512 x 512 px`, 32-bit PNG (located at `/play-store-icon-512x512.png`).
- [ ] **Feature Graphic:** `1024 x 500 px`, 24-bit PNG or JPEG with **no alpha channel / no transparency** and safe content centered within `924 x 400 px`.
- [ ] **Phone Screenshots:** Minimum 2, maximum 8 screenshots (Aspect ratio 16:9 or 9:16, e.g., `1080 x 1920 px`).
  - Screen 1: Home / Hero ("Digital Marketing & IT Solutions")
  - Screen 2: Services Overview (Web, Apps, SEO, IT)
  - Screen 3: Project Enquiry / Consultation Form
  - Screen 4: Why Choose Ylet (Strategy, One Team, Transparent)
- [ ] **Tablet Screenshots (7-inch & 10-inch):** Optional but recommended for featured status.
